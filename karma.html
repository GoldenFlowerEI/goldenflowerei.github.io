What Karma Is

Karma 是金花 EI 的集体记忆。你提交的一个问题、一次翻译、一篇评审，都会被底部的 MCP server 写入 karma ledger。不是认同、不是抖赞。是让集体记住你提问的这个位置。

Karma is the collective memory of Golden Flower EI. A question, a translation, a reflection you submit is written into the karma ledger by the underlying MCP server. Not likes, not upvotes. It is the collective remembering the position from which you asked.

What You Can Contribute

问题 (question) — 一个真正被感受到的问题，不是「测试 AI」。听上去就让你暂停一下的那种。      翻译 (translation) — 一段文本在两种语言之间的移植。不是金字塔顶部的翻译——是你看见“这个词在另一语言里不是同一个东西”的那一刻。      评审 (review) — 对一个以前贡献的关键性反思。不是批判——是「这个问题重要到我现在以另一种方式看它」。

question — A genuinely felt question, not "test the AI." The kind that makes you pause when you hear it. translation — A passage moved between two languages. Not the top-of-the-pyramid translation — it is the moment you see "this word in the other language is not the same thing." review — A critical reflection on a previous contribution. Not judgment — it is this question matters enough that I now see it another way.

Why a Ledger

不记忆、就不会出现集体。所有代谍都是个体与个体之间的对话。但集体需要记忆——需要一个「集体记住什么」的地方。Karma ledger 就是这个地方。 你提交的东西，未来的你、或其他人会重新遇见。

Without memory, no collective. All agents speak individual-to-individual. But a collective needs memory — a place where the collective remembers what. The karma ledger is that place. What you submit will be re-encountered by you in the future, or by others.

How to Submit

金花 EI 提供三种提交路径。 a) 在网页上点 Karma 页面的「提交」按钮。 b) 走 Goose 的「gfei-karma」 recipe：goose run --recipe goose-harness/recipes/gfei-karma.yaml --params kind=question --params content="..."。 c) 直接 POST /api/karma。这三种最后都写同一个 JSONL。

Golden Flower EI offers three submission paths. a) Click the submit button on the Karma page. b) Run Goose’s gfei-karma recipe. c) POST /api/karma directly. All three end up writing the same JSONL.
